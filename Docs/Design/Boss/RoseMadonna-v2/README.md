### 인게임 도트

Use case stylized-concept. Create exactly four closely matching idle animation poses of the same original Rose Window Madonna for a dark Gothic side-scroller, in a single equally spaced horizontal row with very large transparent gaps and common bottom baseline. Reference 1 is the existing boss to revise: preserve character identity, female stone sculpture bound to the wall by red roots instead of legs, dark recess for the separate heart low at the bottom, and 140×220 logical pixel silhouette. Reference 2 is the game's existing combat sprites and palette: match their real chunky opaque pixel clusters, spare shading, sharp stepped edges and dark earthy Gothic color. Change the proportions clearly: an ADULT woman in a restrained anime/mōe style, with a roughly 20% larger head than reference 1, much clearer slightly larger expressive eyes, delicate small nose, softer oval ash-porcelain cheeks and an older young-adult woman's expression, a small sweep of charcoal-stone hair under the veil. More legible and charismatic in actual game size, with a little sad warmth in her expression. She retains a naturally full large bust beneath an OPAQUE dark diagonally folded habit, with balanced shoulder breadth and a shorter, natural waist; do not make her a child, chibi, caricature or pin-up. Increase clothing damage significantly: one veil edge is deeply torn with very uneven strips and at least one visible hole, ragged ripped sleeve flaps, a diagonal split through the outer side of the gown with gray-black opaque lining under it, and several long sharply broken sections of the lower mantle with negative space between their ends. Make the silhouette tears big and readable, with few light gray rim pixels on the frayed edges. Entire body remains clothed, no cleavage or exposed torso. Wall-bound head, bust, shoulders and clasped hands hold completely steady; only the dark wine-maroon root base expands and contracts subtly in four chronological phases. Keep a dark empty square recess in the lower base for a separate movable heart sprite, centered about 36 logical px above bottom. Authentic production 2D pixel art, approximately 18–28 earthy palette shades, contiguous solid color clusters, minimal dither, no rendering gradients or photoreal stone. Pale face and edges, neutral stone-gray robe, charcoal and subdued dark burgundy in roots. No wall, altar, scene, floor, shadow, text, glow, floating icon or frames. Exactly four upright frontal figures of the same design, full body including roots visible in each cell, 100% clean transparent background.

### 등장 컷

Create one transparent full-figure Gothic game boss introduction overlay, in the style of a high quality stylized 3D anime game cut-in. The reference is a sprite sheet of one revised character; preserve exactly the same character and costume, and show only ONE figure. Adult Rose Window Madonna, a young adult woman with a gentle mournful expression and larger expressive gray-brown eyes, slight charcoal-stone fringe under the veil, slim oval ash-porcelain face, prominent adult proportions and a full bust under opaque diagonal charcoal and warm-gray drapery. The feminine face is stylized anime with elegant clean brows and restrained facial shadows. No chibi, no child-like face or proportions, no glossy photo look. She is an uncanny tall saint carved out of worn stone, with folded hands at waist. Her veil is split asymmetrically with distinct holes, her sleeves have heavy hanging torn strips, her torn outer mantle reveals dark opaque cloth layers underneath. Keep every major garment tear visible with light catching the torn edges, and preserve her deeply layered black-gray folds. From the waist down she fuses into a vertical black-maroon mass of ropy tendrils and woven roots, no legs or feet. Low heart recess has a small closed shell. Show her at a slight three-quarter angle so head, upper body and roots all read, with soft desaturated moon-gray lighting and a faint warmer edge reflecting a candle. Art direction of old dark cathedral illustration, stylized hand-painted materials with lightly distressed stone, not hyper-real. Adult, fully covered, dignified and eerie. One figure isolated with generous margins at top and bottom, genuine transparent alpha. No setting, no pedestal, no floor, no typography, no glow, no shadow behind, no backdrop or effects.
